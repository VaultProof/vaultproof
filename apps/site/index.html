01 / FLOW

Three moves. No key ever lives whole.

01 scan

The init CLI walks your repo, parses .env files, and matches supported provider patterns. Git history gets the last 10 commits.

npx @vaultproof/init scan .
02 split

Each live key is split locally into two encrypted shares. Share 1 is sealed at rest on our servers. Share 2 is bound to your developer key.

key = share_1 ⊕ share_2
03 proxy

Your SDK keeps its existing interface. Requests hit api.vaultproof.dev, the worker reconstructs the key in RAM for ≈100ms, forwards, zeroes.

OPENAI_BASE_URL=vaultproof.dev
02 / COMPARISON

How we differ from the usual suspects.

Other tools secure where the key lives. We change the fact that a whole key ever has to live anywhere.
