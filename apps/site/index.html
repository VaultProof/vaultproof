Your keys are protected by math, not promises

Not access policies. Not pinky swears. Cryptographic guarantees.

Split on arrival

The moment you paste your API key, it's split into encrypted pieces in your browser. The original is destroyed instantly. Half a key is useless to anyone.

Prove you own it, without showing it

Zero-knowledge proofs verify you're the owner without ever revealing the key. No shared secrets. No single point of compromise. Your developer key alone can't reconstruct anything.

Gone in 100ms

Your key exists in memory for about 100 milliseconds. The API call fires, the response comes back, and all key material is wiped. Nothing to steal.

Split. Proxy. Zero.

Three steps between you and keys that can't be stolen.

1

Split

Paste your API key. It's instantly split into encrypted pieces right in your browser. The original key is destroyed before it ever leaves your device.

2

Proxy

Your app calls VaultProof. We make the API call for you. The key is reassembled in memory for about 100ms, then wiped. You get the response. The key is gone.

3

Zero

All key material is erased from memory the instant the call completes. Nothing is stored, cached, or logged. There's nothing left to steal.
