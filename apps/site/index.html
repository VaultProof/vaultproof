How It Works

✄

1. Key is split instantly

You paste your API key. It's immediately split into two shares using Shamir Secret Sharing. The full key is destroyed. This happens in your browser — it never leaves your device whole.

🔒

2. Shares stored separately

Share 1 goes to our vault (encrypted). Share 2 stays on your device. Neither share alone reveals anything — half a key is cryptographic garbage.

⚡

3. Ephemeral reconstruction

When you make an API call, a zero-knowledge proof authorizes the request. Shares combine for ~100ms, the call is made, memory is zeroed. Every access is cryptographically logged.
