vaultproof.dev admin boundary

VaultProof Admin

Staff manages enterprise customers on the separate admin host. Enterprise customers sign in on the enterprise host and land inside their own business workspace.

Staff Admin

Create businesses, invite business admins, set SSO, and copy per-business login links from the employee-only admin console.

Staff sign in Admin console

Enterprise Customers

Business users sign in on the enterprise customer system. Each business can use its own login link with the organization id or configured SSO domain.

Customer sign in Enterprise site

System Split

vaultproof.dev is the root/B2C system. admin.vaultproof.dev is the staff admin system. enterprise.vaultproof.dev is the enterprise customer system.

Security Rule

No staff/admin action should expose provider keys, Supabase service-role keys, OAuth client secrets, SAML metadata secrets, origin-lock values, or executor signing material in the browser.
