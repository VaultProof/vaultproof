Report Abuse

If you believe an API key stored on VaultProof was stolen, leaked, or is being used without authorization, report it here.

For urgent requests, email abuse@vaultproof.dev directly.

What Happens Next

  • We review all abuse reports within 24 hours
  • We investigate the claim by cross-referencing access logs and provider reports
  • If the report is confirmed, we immediately revoke the key and terminate the associated account
  • We notify the reporter of the outcome when possible
