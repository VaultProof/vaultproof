Features

What does VaultProof provide?

Three layers of cryptographic protection, zero config required.

Shamir Secret Splitting

Your key is split into encrypted shares the instant you store it. No single server ever holds the complete key — not even us.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Noir ZK circuits prove your app is authorized without revealing the key. Cryptographic guarantees backed by math, not promises.

Transparent Proxy

Change one URL. Your app talks to VaultProof, we talk to the AI provider. The real key exists in memory for ~50ms, then it's gone.

How it works

Three steps. One line of code.

1

Store your key

Paste your API key. It's Shamir-split in your browser — the full key never leaves your machine. Encrypted shares are stored server-side.

2

Change your base URL

baseURL: "https://api.vaultproof.dev/v1/openai"

3

Done. Your key is safe.

Every call is proxied through VaultProof. The real key is reconstructed for ~50ms, used, and zeroed from memory. Your code never sees it.
